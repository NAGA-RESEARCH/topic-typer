Active Learning Meets Muscle Memory.

TopicTyper is an open-learning experiment created to merge cognitive knowledge acquisition with motor-skill typing fluency.

📖 The TopicTyper Philosophy

Traditional typing tutors subject learners to repetitive, sterile word drills or artificial sentence fragments. While this trains isolated finger movements, it completely disconnects practice from real intellectual engagement and curiosity.

TopicTyper bridges motor practice with encyclopedic exploration. By streaming live summaries from Wikipedia across astrophysics, history, quantum mechanics, artificial intelligence, and philosophy, typing transforms into an engaging knowledge-absorption habit. Every keystroke reinforces both motor agility and conceptual understanding.

Active Knowledge Absorption
Read and internalize genuine encyclopedic content as you type, engaging both hemispheres through tactile motor feedback.
Distraction-Free Focus Arena
Spacious 94% reading-first view, sharp 2px glowing vertical I-beam caret with zero letter covering, and smart idle detection.
Weak Keys Telemetry Engine
Real-time keystroke tracking, visual QWERTY heatmap color-coding, and custom drills targeted at your slowest fingers.

🔬 Naga Research Lab

Naga Research Lab is an independent research and software engineering initiative dedicated to developing open-source developer tools, cognitive augmentations, and interactive learning systems.

Our core mission is to create software that respects user attention, operates with pure client-side efficiency, and promotes deeper understanding. TopicTyper was engineered with zero heavy third-party framework dependencies, runs entirely in the browser with local privacy, and provides instantaneous response times.
